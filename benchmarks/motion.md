## Minimum-snap trajectories

Degree-7 segments, snap minimised as an equality-constrained QP: `min cᵀHc s.t. C·c = b`, `H`
block-diagonal in `Dᵢ⁻⁷·Q`, `C` pinning segment endpoints and matching velocity, acceleration and
jerk at each joint. multicalc eliminates the known values and solves the reduced `3(M − 1)` system;
the generator forms the full `8M` KKT system `[[2H, Cᵀ], [C, 0]]·[c; λ] = [0; b]`, which is worse
conditioned — hence the looser tolerance on coefficients than on the states they produce.

## Motion profiles

Seven-phase S-curve and three-phase trapezoidal OTG, rest to rest. multicalc solves the phase
durations in closed form from `v_peak = min(v_max, v_reach)`; the generator recovers them with SLSQP
under the same displacement and limit constraints, then integrates the jerk schedule with DOP853.
Shared only the phase-lengths-to-displacement map. Durations at `1e-7` (optimizer), states at `1e-9`
(integrator).
