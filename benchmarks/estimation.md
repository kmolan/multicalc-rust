# Estimation accuracy

_Table below generated by `cargo run -p multicalc-qa --bin gen_accuracy_tables`; do not edit it by hand._

Every row runs a fixed measurement sequence through predict/update and compares the final estimate
against [FilterPy](https://github.com/rlabbe/filterpy), bar the last two: numpy and scipy instead.

In the equations, `x` is the state vector, `u` the control input, and `x⁻` the predicted state.
`F` is the state-transition matrix (`x⁻ = F·x`), `H` the measurement model mapping state to
measurement, and `h` its nonlinear counterpart (`z = h(x)`). `f` is the matching counterpart on the
prediction side (`x⁻ = f(x)`), used where the motion itself curves: the coordinated-turn rows carry
the state `[x, y, θ, v, ω]` (position, heading, forward speed, and turn rate) one tick along a
turning arc. `α` is the unscented filter's spread setting.
