## Distance transform

Felzenszwalb–Huttenlocher (*Distance Transforms of Sampled Functions*, 2004): two separable
one-dimensional lower-envelope passes over the parabolas `f(q) + (x − q)²` in squared cell units,
rooted and scaled to metres. O(cells), no priority queue.
`scipy.ndimage.distance_transform_edt` computes the same quantity by a different exact method, so
`1e-12` is floating-point noise rather than algorithmic slack.

## Costmap inflation

The nav2 formulation over that field: lethal within the inscribed radius,
`254·exp(−k·(d − inscribed))` out to the inflation radius, zero beyond. Both sides truncate to
`u8`, so the comparison is exact.

## Likelihood field

The endpoint model of *Probabilistic Robotics* §6.4: each beam's endpoint projected from the pose,
its obstacle distance read bilinearly from the field, scored `ln((1 − p)·exp(−d²/2σ²) + p/r_max)`,
with an endpoint off the field contributing the noise term alone. The total sums 32 logarithms,
hence `1e-10` against `1e-12` for a single lookup.
