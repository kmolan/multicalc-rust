# Dynamics accuracy

_Table below generated by `cargo run -p multicalc-qa --bin gen_accuracy_tables`; do not edit it by hand._

A single free body's accelerations against
[MuJoCo](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco)'s own solve: one case spinning with nothing
applied, one tilted and spinning under a wrench. Each is worked out a second time in numpy before
the fixture is written, so a frame read the wrong way round fails at generation time.

`m` is the mass, `c` the centre of mass, `I` the rotational inertia about it, `R` the orientation,
`ω` the angular rate, `f` and `τ` the applied wrench, and `g` gravity.

Inverse dynamics, the joint-space inertia matrix and forward dynamics against
[Pinocchio](https://github.com/stack-of-tasks/pinocchio)'s `rnea`, `crba` and `aba`, on a two-link
pendulum and the Menagerie Franka Panda over 128 random states each. MuJoCo's `mj_inverse` is
checked against Pinocchio first, with damping, friction loss and joint stiffness zeroed so neither
library's passive-force convention enters. Neither oracle carries joint friction — `rnea` has none,
MuJoCo solves `frictionloss` as a constraint — so `armature⊙q̈ + damping⊙q̇ + frictionloss⊙sign(q̇)`
rides on Pinocchio's rigid-body torque, which the fixtures carry separately so the two are checked
apart.

`H` is the joint-space inertia matrix, `C` the Coriolis and centrifugal terms, `G` gravity, and
`armature`, `damping` and `frictionloss` the per-joint values the model states.
