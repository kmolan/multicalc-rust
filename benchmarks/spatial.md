# Spatial accuracy

_Table below generated by `cargo run -p multicalc-qa --bin gen_accuracy_tables`; do not edit it by hand._

Reading a motion or a force from a different frame, the two ways a moving frame carries one of them
along, and what a body's mass distribution does to a motion. The numbers to match come from
Pinocchio, which lays these six numbers out straight-line part first, exactly as this crate does, so
the two line up one to one with nothing reordered on the way in. Each golden is worked out a second
time in numpy before it is saved, so a part read the wrong way round is caught before the file is
written.

In the equations, `R` and `p` are the turn and the shift between the two frames, `v` and `ω` how fast
something is moving and turning, `f` and `τ` the push and the turn applied, `m` the mass, `c` the
point a body balances on, and `I_c` how hard it is to spin about that point.
