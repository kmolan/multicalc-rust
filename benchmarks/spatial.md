# Spatial accuracy

_Table below generated by `cargo run -p multicalc-qa --bin gen_accuracy_tables`; do not edit it by hand._

Plücker transforms of twists and wrenches, the spatial cross products `×` and `×*`, power, and
spatial-inertia algebra, against Pinocchio. Pinocchio orders a spatial vector linear part first, as
this crate does, so the components compare one to one with no reorder. The generator computes each
golden again in numpy before it writes the fixture, so a wrong convention fails at generation time.

In the equations, `R` and `p` are the rotation and translation between the frames, `v` and `ω` the
linear and angular velocity, `f` and `τ` the force and torque, `m` the mass, `c` the centre of mass,
and `I_c` the rotational inertia about `c`.
